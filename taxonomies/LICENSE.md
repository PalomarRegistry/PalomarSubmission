# Taxonomy data licensing

`msc2020-codes.json` is derived from the MSC2020 classification jointly
published by Mathematical Reviews and zbMATH and is distributed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

`arxiv-categories.json` is derived from arXiv's public
[category taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/category_taxonomy). The repository's MIT
license applies to the surrounding validation software, not to third-party
taxonomy data.
